# 2024 Lok Sabha Manifestos: Party Stances on Concrete Policy Statements (Manifesto Matcher)

**Method.** I downloaded the BJP and INC English manifesto PDFs, extracted the text with `pdftotext -layout`, split it by page and searched every page for keywords. Page numbers below are the **printed page numbers** in each document. In the BJP PDF the printed number is the PDF page minus 3, and in the INC PDF it is the PDF page minus 1. I checked this offset against the page footers.

- **BJP "Modi Ki Guarantee – Sankalp Patra" 2024 (English, 76 PDF pages).** The official URL linked from the BJP site is https://www.bjp.org/files/inline-documents/Modi-Ki-Guarantee-Sankalp-Patra-English_0.pdf (listing page: https://www.bjp.org/manifesto). bjp.org timed out from this environment, so I verified the text against an identical-named mirror of the official file hosted by Oneindia: https://www.oneindia.com/downloads/2024/4/Modi-Ki-Guarantee-Sankalp-Patra-English_011.pdf.
- **INC "Nyay Patra" 2024 (English, 48 PDF pages).** Official file: https://manifesto.inc.in/assets/Congress-Manifesto-English-2024-Dyoxp_4E.pdf.
- "Silent" means a keyword search of the whole document found no commitment on that statement. In the BJP PDF a few pages extract as overprinted, garbled text (for example PDF pp. 24 and 58). A "silent" result for BJP therefore carries a small risk of missing a mention. I flag this where it matters.

## What does the BJP "Sankalp Patra" 2024 say on each statement (page numbers)?

### Takeaway
The BJP manifesto clearly supports UCC, One Nation One Election, implementation of the women's reservation Act, the paper-leak law and free electricity through rooftop solar. It does not mention a legal MSP guarantee, caste census, Old Pension Scheme, Agnipath, J&K statehood, apprenticeship rights, a cash transfer to women or disinvestment.

### Cited Findings
- **MSP (Silent on legal guarantee):** "Increase in MSP – We have ensured unprecedented increase in MSP for major crops, and we will continue to increase MSP from time to time." (p.22). There is no mention of a legal guarantee. — [BJP Sankalp Patra 2024](https://www.bjp.org/files/inline-documents/Modi-Ki-Guarantee-Sankalp-Patra-English_0.pdf) (verified via [mirror](https://www.oneindia.com/downloads/2024/4/Modi-Ki-Guarantee-Sankalp-Patra-English_011.pdf))
- **One Nation One Election (Supports):** Heading "Making One Nation, One Election a Reality". The text reads approximately: "We have set up a High Powered Committee to examine the issues of conducting simultaneous election and will work towards the implementation of the recommendations of the Committee." The same page also has "Introducing Common Electoral Roll" (p.55). The extracted text on this page is overprinted and garbled, so this quote is reconstructed and should be checked visually against the PDF. — [BJP Sankalp Patra 2024](https://www.bjp.org/files/inline-documents/Modi-Ki-Guarantee-Sankalp-Patra-English_0.pdf); [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/bjps-manifesto-promises-ucc-implementation-one-nation-one-election-enn24041401814) confirms that the manifesto includes ONOE.
- **UCC (Supports):** "Bringing Uniform Civil Code – Article 44 of the Constitution lists Uniform Civil Code as one of the Directive Principles of State Policy. BJP believes that there cannot be gender equality till such time Bharat adopts a Uniform Civil Code, which protects the rights of all women, and the BJP reiterates its stand to draw a Uniform Civil Code, drawing upon the best traditions and harmonising them with the modern times." (p.54) — [BJP Sankalp Patra 2024](https://www.bjp.org/files/inline-documents/Modi-Ki-Guarantee-Sankalp-Patra-English_0.pdf)
- **Caste census (Silent):** The words "caste" and "census" do not appear in the extracted text. — [BJP Sankalp Patra 2024](https://www.bjp.org/files/inline-documents/Modi-Ki-Guarantee-Sankalp-Patra-English_0.pdf)
- **Old Pension Scheme (Silent):** The only "pension" mention is a past achievement (Atal Pension Yojana, PM Shramyogi Maandhan, p.7). There is no OPS or NPS commitment. — same source
- **Agnipath (Silent):** No match for "Agni". — same source
- **Women's reservation (Supports implementation, no timeline):** "Implementing the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam – We have enacted the long-awaited Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam. We will systematically implement this to ensure representation for women in the Parliament and State Legislatures." (p.16) — same source
- **Paper leak law (Supports):** "Implementing Law to Prevent Paper Leaks – We have already enacted a strict law to stop misconduct in recruitment exams across the country. We will strictly implement this law to ensure strict punishment to those playing [with the future of youth]…" (p.17). The achievements list on p.6 also cites the "Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act 2024 to prevent paper leaks". — same source
- **Free electricity (Supports, via solar):** "Zero Electricity Bill – We will provide free electricity to poor households under PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana." (p.12). There is also "Implementing PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana" (p.49). — same source
- **Cash transfer to women (Silent):** No unconditional cash transfer to women. The nearest item is "Three Crore Lakhpati Didis … We will now empower three crore rural women to become Lakhpati Didis" (p.15), which is an SHG livelihood scheme and not a cash transfer. — same source
- **GST reform (Limited / Silent on structural reform):** "GST Portal – We will further simplify the GST portal to increase accessibility to users, especially MSMEs and small traders." (p.31). GST is otherwise mentioned only as a past achievement (p.9). — same source
- **J&K statehood (Silent):** The only J&K reference is the achievement "Revoked Article 370 leading to a significant reduction in violence in Jammu and Kashmir" (p.8). There is no statehood commitment. — same source
- **Apprenticeship right (Silent):** No match for "apprentice". The manifesto instead promises to expand skills training and to double the Mudra loan limit to Rs 20 lakh (p.19). — same source
- **PSU disinvestment (Silent):** No match for "disinvest", "privati", "PSU" or "public sector". — same source

### Inferences
- For statements 1, 4, 5, 6, 10, 12 and 13, BJP should be coded "silent", not "opposes", because the manifesto takes no explicit position. BJP's opposition to some of these (for example Agnipath rollback) is known from outside the manifesto and should not be coded from this source.

### Gaps
- I could not reach bjp.org directly (timeouts). The text was verified from a third-party mirror of a file with the same name. Someone should do a visual check of p.55 (ONOE) against the official PDF because of the garbled extraction.
- Silent findings rest on keyword search. A few overprinted pages may hide terms.

## What does the INC "Nyay Patra" 2024 say on each statement (page numbers)?

### Takeaway
The Nyay Patra explicitly supports a legal MSP guarantee, caste census, scrapping Agnipath, immediate women's reservation, paper-leak fast-track courts, the Mahalakshmi cash transfer, a GST Council redesign, J&K statehood and a Right to Apprenticeship. It explicitly rejects One Nation One Election. It is silent on UCC as such, on Old Pension Scheme, on free electricity and on disinvestment.

### Cited Findings
- **MSP (Supports):** "Congress will give a legal guarantee to the Minimum Support Prices (MSP) announced by the government every year, as recommended by the Swaminathan Commission." It also says the CACP "will be made a statutory body" (p.17, Farmers). — [INC Nyay Patra 2024](https://manifesto.inc.in/assets/Congress-Manifesto-English-2024-Dyoxp_4E.pdf)
- **One Nation One Election (Opposes):** "We reject the 'one nation one election' idea and we promise that elections to the Lok Sabha and the State Assemblies will be held as and when they are due in accordance with the Constitution." (p.22, Samvidhanik Nyay) — same source
- **UCC (Silent):** "Uniform Civil Code" does not appear. On personal laws: "Congress will ensure that, like every citizen, minorities have the freedom of choice of dress, food, language and personal laws" and "We will encourage reform of personal laws. Such reform must be undertaken with the participation and consent of the communities concerned." (p.8) — same source
- **Caste census (Supports):** "Congress will conduct a nation-wide Socio-Economic and Caste Census to enumerate the castes and sub-castes and their socio-economic conditions. Based on the data, we will strengthen the agenda for affirmative action." (p.6). Fishing communities "will be enumerated in the caste census" (p.19). — same source
- **Old Pension Scheme (Silent):** No match for "Old Pension", "OPS", "NPS" or "National Pension". Pension mentions cover NSAP pensions for the elderly and disabled (p.9) and OROP for the armed forces (p.39). — same source
- **Agnipath (Supports scrapping):** "Abolish the Agnipath programme and direct the Armed Forces (Army, Navy, Air Force and Coast Guard) to resume normal recruitment to achieve the full sanctioned strength." (p.30). Also: "Congress will scrap the Agnipath Scheme and return to the normal recruitment processes followed by the Army, Navy and Air Force…" (p.38). — same source
- **Women's reservation (Supports immediate implementation):** The Act's provisions mean reservation would "come into operation only beyond 2029. Congress will delete the sinister provisions and bring the Amendment Act into force immediately. The one-third reservation for women will be applied to State Assemblies that will be elected in the next round of Assembly elections in 2025. The one-third reservation for women will also be applied to the Lok Sabha that will be elected in 2029." (p.16) — same source
- **Paper leaks (Supports):** "We will provide fast-track courts to adjudicate cases of leaking question papers for job examinations and provide monetary compensation to the victims." (p.11) — same source
- **Free electricity (Silent):** There is no free-power promise. The only electricity item is to "implement renewable energy schemes that will make the panchayats or municipality self-sufficient in electricity as far as possible" (p.33). — same source
- **Cash transfer to women (Supports):** "Congress resolves to launch a Mahalakshmi scheme to provide ₹1 lakh per year to every poor Indian family as an unconditional cash transfer." The amount goes to the oldest woman of the household, and "Absent a woman, it will be transferred to the account of the oldest member of the family" (p.15). — same source
- **GST reform (Supports):** "GST will not be levied on agricultural inputs." and "The GST Council will be re-designed. It will be the final authority on policy and on all matters relating to GST." (p.31, Taxation). It also promises to devolve "a share of GST revenues, directly to panchayats and municipalities" (p.35). — same source
- **J&K statehood (Supports):** "We will immediately restore full statehood to Jammu and Kashmir." It also commits to "grant full statehood to Puducherry" and to amend the Sixth Schedule for Ladakh (p.36). — same source
- **Apprenticeship (Supports):** "Congress guarantees a new Right to Apprenticeship Act to provide a one year apprenticeship with a private or a public sector company to every diploma holder or college graduate below the age of 25. Apprentices will get ₹1 lakh a year." (p.11) — same source
- **Disinvestment (Silent):** No match for "disinvest" or "privati". The general line is "an open economy in which economic growth will be driven by the private sector complemented by a strong and viable public sector" (p.27). It also promises to "abolish the contractualisation of regular jobs in the government and public sector enterprises" (p.6). — same source

### Inferences
- The INC personal-laws language (p.8) supports reform with community consent. That is not the same as opposing UCC, so it should be coded "silent" on the UCC statement and not "opposes".

### Gaps
- None for the listed items. All INC stances were verified from the primary PDF.
