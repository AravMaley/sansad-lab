Research date: 2026-10-27. Sources include PRS, Wikipedia (an aggregator, used only to cross-check), legal news (LiveLaw, Verdictum, SC Observer), and national news outlets. PIB pages returned HTTP 403 and could not be fetched directly. The PRS DPDP bill page URL returned 404.

## Q1. Women's Reservation: Constitution (106th Amendment) Act 2023 / Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam

### Takeaway
The bill was introduced on 19 Sep 2023 and passed the Lok Sabha 454–2 on 20 Sep and the Rajya Sabha 214–0 on 21 Sep 2023. It was gazetted after assent on 28 Sep 2023 and brought into force on 16 Apr 2026. It still does not operate: seats are reserved only after the first census following commencement and the delimitation based on it. On 17 Apr 2026 a government attempt to fast-track it for 2029 (the 131st Amendment Bill) failed in the Lok Sabha, 298–230.

### Cited Findings
**Plain-language summary (for the game):** The amendment reserves one-third of seats in the Lok Sabha, state assemblies and the Delhi assembly for women. This includes one-third of the seats already reserved for SCs and STs. It starts only after a new census is published and constituencies are redrawn, and it lasts 15 years, which Parliament can extend. Reserved seats rotate after each delimitation. — [PRS Bill Track](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-twenty-eighth-amendment-bill-2023)

**Key provisions**
- One-third of total seats in the LS, state assemblies and the Delhi assembly are reserved for women. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-twenty-eighth-amendment-bill-2023)
- One-third of the SC/ST-reserved seats are also reserved for women. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-twenty-eighth-amendment-bill-2023)
- Takes effect after the census conducted after commencement is published, followed by delimitation. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-twenty-eighth-amendment-bill-2023)
- Lasts 15 years, extendable by a law of Parliament. Rotation is set by a law of Parliament after each delimitation. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-twenty-eighth-amendment-bill-2023)
- Media reported that it covers about 181 Lok Sabha seats (33% of 543). — [DT Next / IANS](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/national/parl-passes-womens-reservation-bill-after-unanimous-support-in-rs-737718)
- There is no OBC sub-quota. (This is common knowledge, but no fetched source states it explicitly; verify before use.)

**Dates and votes**
- Introduced as the Constitution (128th Amendment) Bill in the Lok Sabha on 19 Sep 2023, the first business in the new Parliament building. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-twenty-eighth-amendment-bill-2023)
- Lok Sabha passed it on 20 Sep 2023 by division, **454 Ayes, 2 Noes**. Both Noes were AIMIM MPs. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fhistoric-womens-reservation-bill-clears-parliament-hurdle-2696190); [LiveLaw live updates](https://livelaw.in/top-stories/womens-reservation-bill-live-updates-from-rajya-sabha-238294). Scroll printed "452", which looks like a typo. — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1056364/parliament-clears-womens-reservation-bill)
- Rajya Sabha passed it on 21 Sep 2023, **214 Ayes, 0 Noes** (unanimous). — [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/top-stories/womens-reservation-bill-live-updates-from-rajya-sabha-238294); [Organiser](https://organiser.org/2023/09/22/197210/bharat/womens-reservation-bill-passed-unanimousously-in-rajya-sabha-dhankhar-says-historic-achievement/). The Quint said "over 215". — [The Quint](https://thequint.com/amp/story/gender/womens-reservation-bill-passed-in-rajya-sabha)
- Committee: the 2023 Bill was **not referred** to any committee. Earlier versions were: the 1996 Bill went to a Joint Committee, and the 2008 Bill went to the Standing Committee on Personnel, Public Grievances, Law and Justice. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-twenty-eighth-amendment-bill-2023)
- Presidential assent: President Droupadi Murmu assented, and the Act was gazetted on **28 Sep 2023**. That notification said a separate notification would set the date of enforcement. — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/women-reservation-act-2023-comes-into-force-notification-enn26041700047)

**Implementation status (2026)**
- A Law Ministry gazette notification appointed **16 Apr 2026** as the date the Act came into force, more than 900 days after passage. — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/women-reservation-act-2023-comes-into-force-notification-enn26041700047); [The Wire](https://thewire.in/government/modi-govt-enforces-2023-womens-reservation-act-more-than-900-days-after-it-was-passed); [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/1092175/womens-reservation-act-2023-comes-into-force-amid-parliament-debate-on-amendments)
- Being in force does not make the reservation operational. Current Houses are unaffected, and reservation applies only after delimitation. Congress called the timing "absolutely bizarre". — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/women-reservation-act-2023-comes-into-force-notification-enn26041700047)
- The **Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026** was introduced on 16 Apr 2026 by Law Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal. It would have operationalised the quota using delimitation based on the 2011 census, so it could apply in 2029. It came with the Delimitation Bill 2026, which would raise the cap on Lok Sabha seats from 550 to 850. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitution_(131st_Amendment)_Bill,_2026)
- The 131st Amendment Bill **failed in the Lok Sabha on 17 Apr 2026, 298 Ayes to 230 Noes**. With 528 present, it needed 352 for a two-thirds majority. Reports call this the first failed constitutional amendment under the Modi government. The government then withdrew the Delimitation Bill. — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/defeat-of-the-constitution-131st-amendment-bill-2026); [The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/article/politics/major-setback-modi-govts-constitution-amendment-bill-delimitation-fails-in-lok-sabha); [ISAS NUS](https://www.isas.nus.edu.sg/papers/womens-quota-bill-defeated-in-india-amid-row-over-delimitation/)
- The census reference date is reported as 1 Mar 2027, so realistic implementation may fall after 2029. — [Vidhi / aggregated explainer results](https://vidhilegalpolicy.in/research/beyond-womens-reservation/) (secondary; verify against the census notification)

**Arguments FOR (attributed)**
- It fulfils a demand pending since the 1996 Bill. The cross-party near-unanimity (454–2, 214–0) shows broad consensus. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-constitution-one-hundred-twenty-eighth-amendment-bill-2023); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fhistoric-womens-reservation-bill-clears-parliament-hurdle-2696190)
- Then Rajya Sabha Chairman Jagdeep Dhankhar called its passage a "historic achievement". — [Organiser](https://organiser.org/2023/09/22/197210/bharat/womens-reservation-bill-passed-unanimousously-in-rajya-sabha-dhankhar-says-historic-achievement/)
- Government (Amit Shah, April 2026): blocking the fast-track amendment denies women their quota, and he said India's women would not "forgive" the opposition. — [The Wire](https://m.thewire.in/article/politics/major-setback-modi-govts-constitution-amendment-bill-delimitation-fails-in-lok-sabha)
- Linking the quota to fresh delimitation lets reserved seats be drawn on current population data, which is the government's rationale for the census trigger. (This is an inference from the Act's structure. I found no fetched quote stating it.)

**Arguments AGAINST (attributed)**
- Petitioner Jaya Thakur (Congress leader): the census-and-delimitation precondition indefinitely delays a right, so it should be struck down and the quota applied immediately. Her 2025 plea says the condition violates Articles 14, 19 and 21. — [Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/court-updates/supreme-court/women-reservation-106-amendment-dr-jaya-thakur-v-union-of-india-wpc-11812023-1564454); [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/amp/en/bharat/sc-seeks-centres-response-on-implementing-womens-reservation-in-parliament-enn25111001856)
- AIMIM cast the only 2 Noes in the Lok Sabha. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fhistoric-womens-reservation-bill-clears-parliament-hurdle-2696190). Their reason, as commonly reported, was the absence of OBC and Muslim women sub-quotas. This is not confirmed in the fetched text; verify.
- Opposition (April 2026): population-based delimitation would penalise southern states for controlling population, so women's reservation should be fully delinked from delimitation. — [Drishti IAS](https://www.drishtiias.com/daily-updates/daily-news-analysis/defeat-of-the-constitution-131st-amendment-bill-2026)
- Congress: the timing of the April 2026 notification may have been meant to preserve the existing law in case the amendments failed. — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/women-reservation-act-2023-comes-into-force-notification-enn26041700047)

**Court challenges**
- Dr Jaya Thakur v. Union of India, WP(C) 1181/2023. In Nov 2023 Justice Sanjiv Khanna said it would be "very difficult" to strike down the census linkage. The petition was later dismissed as infructuous because it was filed before assent. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-womens-reservation-bill-2024-general-elections-241534); [Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/court-updates/supreme-court/women-reservation-106-amendment-dr-jaya-thakur-v-union-of-india-wpc-11812023-1564454)
- The fresh 2025 petition is Diary No. 30811/2025. In Nov 2025, a bench of Justices B V Nagarathna and R Mahadevan issued notice but said the court cannot issue a mandamus on enforcement, which is the executive's job. — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/amp/en/bharat/sc-seeks-centres-response-on-implementing-womens-reservation-in-parliament-enn25111001856)
- An NFIW petition from 2021 was tagged with these. — [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-one-third-womens-reservation-parliament-state-assemblies-246867)

### Inferences
- For the game, use 454–2 (LS) and 214–0 (RS) as the official counts. The 131st Amendment's 298–230 defeat makes a good "sequel" vote: it shows how a two-thirds requirement works.
- Status as of Oct 2026: the Act is in force but not operational, and it awaits Census 2027 and delimitation.

## Q2. Citizenship (Amendment) Act 2019 (CAA)

### Takeaway
The bill was introduced and passed in the Lok Sabha on 9 Dec 2019 (311–80, vote just after midnight), and passed the Rajya Sabha on 11 Dec 2019 (125–105). It received assent on 12 Dec 2019 and came into force on 10 Jan 2020. Its rules were notified on 11 Mar 2024, and the first certificates under the rules were issued on 15 May 2024. More than 200 Supreme Court petitions (lead: IUML) were listed for final hearing from 5–12 May 2026. I found no reported verdict.

### Cited Findings
**Plain-language summary:** The CAA fast-tracks Indian citizenship for Hindu, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi and Christian migrants from Afghanistan, Bangladesh and Pakistan who entered India on or before 31 Dec 2014. They are not treated as "illegal migrants", and the residency requirement for naturalisation drops to 5 years. Sixth Schedule tribal areas of Assam, Meghalaya, Mizoram and Tripura, and Inner Line Permit areas, are excluded. Muslims are not among the covered groups, and this is the core of the controversy. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-citizenship-amendment-bill-2019)

**Key provisions:** six communities; three countries; cut-off of 31 Dec 2014; naturalisation residency cut to 5 years (the 2016 Bill proposed cutting it from 11 to 6); Sixth Schedule and ILP exemptions. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-citizenship-amendment-bill-2019)

**Dates and votes**
- Introduced in the Lok Sabha and passed there on 9 Dec 2019. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-citizenship-amendment-bill-2019)
- The Lok Sabha division was **311 Ayes, 80 Noes**, with 391 present and voting. The vote came around midnight on 9–10 Dec. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fcitizenship-amendment-bill-cleared-in-parliament-784665.html)
- The Rajya Sabha passed it on 11 Dec 2019, **125 Ayes, 105 Noes**. — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/946543/citizenship-amendment-bill-passed-in-rajya-sabha-with-125-votes-for-105-against); [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/cab-passed-in-rajya-sabha-125-ayes-105-noes). One outlet reported 125–99, which conflicts with the rest; most sources say 125–105. — [India Legal](https://indialegallive.com/top-news-of-the-day/news/125-105-citizenship-amendment-bill-clears-rajya-sabha/)
- In the Rajya Sabha, a motion to refer the bill to a Select Committee was defeated **92–113**. — [BW Businessworld](https://businessworld.in/article/parliament-clears-cab-125-mps-vote-for-bill-in-rs-180252)
- Committee: the 2019 Bill was **not referred**. The predecessor 2016 Bill went to a **Joint Parliamentary Committee**, which reported on 7 Jan 2019. The Lok Sabha passed that Bill on 8 Jan 2019, and it lapsed when the 16th Lok Sabha was dissolved. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-citizenship-amendment-bill-2019)
- Presidential assent: **12 Dec 2019** (President Ram Nath Kovind). Commencement: **10 Jan 2020**. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019)

**Implementation status**
- The Ministry of Home Affairs notified the Citizenship (Amendment) Rules, 2024 on **11 Mar 2024**, with an online application system. — [Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/app-lite/court-updates/supreme-court/will-hear-challenge-citizenship-amendment-act-from-may-5-1608032?hasShare=1); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019)
- The first 14 certificates under the CAA were handed over in Delhi on **15 May 2024**, and over 350 were issued digitally the same day. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019). The user's brief mentioned "14 May"; the source says 15 May.
- I found no official cumulative count of certificates issued.

**Arguments FOR (attributed)**
- Home Minister Amit Shah: the Act gives refuge to religious minorities persecuted in neighbouring Islamic-majority states. He argued that Muslims in Pakistan, Afghanistan and Bangladesh are unlikely to face religious persecution because Islam is the state religion. — [Wikipedia (summarising Shah's statements)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019)
- PM Narendra Modi: "No Indian has anything to worry regarding this act." The Act grants citizenship and takes none away. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019)
- Supporters argue the Act rests on a long-standing commitment to Partition-era minorities. The Joint Parliamentary Committee examined the 2016 version before it lapsed. — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-citizenship-amendment-bill-2019)

**Arguments AGAINST (attributed)**
- IUML and the other lead petitioners: the Act discriminates on religion in granting citizenship, which violates Article 14 and secularism. — [SC Observer](https://www.scobserver.in/reports/citizenship-amendment-act-supreme-court-schedules-final-hearings-in-may-2026/); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/supreme-court-agrees-to-hear-237-petitions-against-caa-on-tuesday-600866)
- Congress: the Act would polarise India and create communal tension. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019)
- Critics, including The Economist, said persecuted Muslim sects such as Ahmadis and Hazaras, and neighbours such as Sri Lanka's Tamils and Myanmar, are left out. They also feared that combined with an NRC, the Act could render Muslim citizens stateless. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019)
- Assam protesters: the Act violates the 1985 Assam Accord (cut-off 1971) and threatens Assam's culture and political rights. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019)
- The Kerala government was the first state to sue, challenging the Act in the Supreme Court. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizenship_(Amendment)_Act,_2019)

**Court challenges**
- Indian Union Muslim League v. Union of India is the lead case among roughly 237–242 petitions pending since Dec 2019. — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/supreme-court-agrees-to-hear-237-petitions-against-caa-on-tuesday-600866); [Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/app-lite/court-updates/supreme-court/will-hear-challenge-citizenship-amendment-act-from-may-5-1608032?hasShare=1)
- In Jan 2020 a bench under CJI Bobde refused a stay without first hearing the Centre, and barred High Courts from hearing CAA cases. — [The Quint](https://fithindi.thequint.com/amp/story/news/india/sc-hearing-citizenship-amendment-act-144-petitions-key-highlights)
- In Mar 2024 a bench under CJI Chandrachud declined to stay the 2024 Rules and sought the Centre's response. Kapil Sibal argued for a stay. SG Tushar Mehta opposed it, questioning the petitioners' locus standi. — [DD News](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/supreme-court-declines-stay-on-caa-sets-deadline-for-government-response/); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/sc-posts-to-9th-april-hearing-on-applications-seeking-stay-on-caa-rules/73723.html)
- In Feb 2026 a bench of CJI Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and Vipul M Pancholi listed the final hearing for **5–12 May 2026**. Petitioners got 1.5 days and the Centre 1 day. — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/sc-to-begin-final-hearing-in-over-200-caa-pleas-on-may-5-enn26021905445); [SC Observer](https://www.scobserver.in/reports/citizenship-amendment-act-supreme-court-schedules-final-hearings-in-may-2026/)

### Inferences
- The Rajya Sabha vote (125–105, a margin of 20) is the most contested of the three bills, which makes it well suited to a "swing vote" scenario.

## Q3. Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP)

### Takeaway
MeitY Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw introduced the bill on 3 Aug 2023. The Lok Sabha passed it on 7 Aug and the Rajya Sabha on 9 Aug 2023, both **by voice vote, with no recorded division**; the opposition had walked out over Manipur. It received assent on 11 Aug 2023 (Act 22 of 2023). The 2023 Bill went to no committee. Its predecessor, the PDP Bill 2019, went to a JPC (report Dec 2021) and was withdrawn in Aug 2022. The DPDP Rules 2025 were notified in Nov 2025 with phased commencement, and full effect comes in May 2027. The Supreme Court issued notice on challenges, including the RTI amendment, on 16 Feb 2026 and referred them to a larger bench, with no stay.

### Cited Findings
**Plain-language summary:** The DPDP Act is India's first general law on digital personal data. Companies and government bodies ("data fiduciaries") must usually get clear consent before using someone's personal data, though some "legitimate uses" are allowed without it. Data must be kept safe, and breaches must be reported. People ("data principals") get rights to access, correct and erase their data and to withdraw consent. Children's data gets extra protection. A Data Protection Board adjudicates complaints and imposes penalties. The government can exempt its own agencies, and the Act narrows the RTI Act's personal-information exemption. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023); [IFF](https://www.internetfreedom.in/supreme-court-issues-notice-on-constitutional-challenge-to-the-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-and-the-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025/)

**Key provisions**
- Consent-based processing, with legitimate-use exceptions such as employment, legal obligations and emergencies. Consent can be withdrawn. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023)
- A Data Protection Board of India adjudicates breaches. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023)
- Processing children's data in ways that harm their well-being, or that involve tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads, is prohibited. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023)
- Exemptions cover legal rights, judicial and regulatory functions, crime prevention, and others. The government can exempt agencies under Section 17. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/supreme-court-issues-notice-on-pleas-challenging-dpdp-acts-impact-on-rti-act/80577.html)
- Cross-border transfer is allowed except to countries the government restricts (a "negative list"). — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023)
- Breaches must be notified to the Board and to affected people. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023)
- Section 44(3) amends RTI Act Section 8(1)(j) to exempt "information which relates to personal information", removing the public-interest override. — [Vajiram & Ravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/dpdp-act-challenge/); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-refers-pleas-challenging-dpdp-act-amendment-to-rti-act-to-larger-bench-523266)
- Section 36 and Rule 23 let the Centre call for information from fiduciaries. — [IFF](https://www.internetfreedom.in/supreme-court-issues-notice-on-constitutional-challenge-to-the-digital-personal-data-protection-act-2023-and-the-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025/)
- Penalties: the widely cited maximum is ₹250 crore per breach, but **not confirmed in the fetched sources**. Wikipedia states a ₹50 crore minimum, which looks questionable. Verify against the Act's Schedule or PRS before use.

**Dates and votes**
- Introduced in the Lok Sabha on 3 Aug 2023 by Ashwini Vaishnaw. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023)
- Passed by the Lok Sabha on 7 Aug 2023 and the Rajya Sabha on 9 Aug 2023. The Rajya Sabha used a **voice vote**, after an opposition walkout over Manipur. No division count exists. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023); [News On AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/rajya-sabha-passes-digital-personal-data-protection-bill-2023); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/news/digital-personal-data-protection-bill-2023-passed-rajya-sabha-180849). IndiaAI gives 8 Aug, which is likely an error. — [IndiaAI](https://indiaai.gov.in/news/digital-personal-data-protection-bill-passed-in-indian-parliament)
- Presidential assent was given on **11 Aug 2023** (Act No. 22 of 2023). — [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/president-grants-assent-to-digital-personal-data-protection-bill-2023)
- Committee: the 2023 Bill was not referred. The **PDP Bill 2019** went to a **Joint Parliamentary Committee**. Its report was tabled on 16 Dec 2021, with 81 amendments and 12 recommendations; sources give 93 recommendations in total. The government withdrew that bill on 3 Aug 2022, citing the extent of the JPC's changes. — [DLA Piper](https://privacymatters.dlapiper.com/2022/08/india-government-withdraws-long-awaited-personal-data-protection-bill/); [Carnegie India](https://carnegieindia.org/2022/08/22/withdrawal-of-proposed-data-protection-law-is-pragmatic-move-pub-87710)

**Implementation status**
- The DPDP Rules 2025 were notified in Nov 2025. The Delhi IT Dept office memorandum gives a 14 Nov 2025 gazette date; law firms say 13 Nov. Phases:
  - Rules 1, 2 and 17–21, covering the Data Protection Board, came into force immediately.
  - Rule 4 (consent managers) takes effect on 14 Nov 2026.
  - Rules 3, 5–16, 22 and 23 (notice, consent, rights, security, breach, children's data, significant data fiduciaries) take effect on 14 May 2027.
  - — [Delhi Govt OM](https://it.delhi.gov.in/sites/default/files/IT/generic_multiple_files/dpdp_om.pdf); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fcentre-notifies-dpdp-rules-implementation-planned-in-phases-spread-over-12-18-months-3798465); [Conventus Law](https://conventuslaw.com/report/india-implementation-to-the-digital-personal-data-protection-act-and-rules/)
- Wikipedia gives the matching Act-commencement dates as 13 Nov 2025, 13 Nov 2026 and 13 May 2027. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023)

**Arguments FOR (attributed)**
- Government/MeitY: India gets its first comprehensive data protection law, with enforceable rights (consent, withdrawal, breach notification) and a dedicated Board. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Personal_Data_Protection_Act,_2023); [News On AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/rajya-sabha-passes-digital-personal-data-protection-bill-2023)
