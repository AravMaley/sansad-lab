# India's 2020 Farm Laws and Their Repeal: Timeline, Parliamentary Record, and Arguments (2020-2024)

Note for the report writer: searches were run in Oct 2026. Most dates were checked against at least one source. PRS bill pages (primary) were fetched for all three bills. Gazette PDFs and Lok Sabha/Rajya Sabha debate records were NOT fetched directly. Where sources disagree, both are shown.

## Q1. When were the ordinances promulgated?

### Takeaway
All three ordinances were promulgated on 5 June 2020, during the COVID-19 lockdown, before Parliament met.

### Cited Findings
- 5 Jun 2020: The Essential Commodities (Amendment) Ordinance, 2020 was promulgated "alongside two other agriculture ordinances" — [PRS: EC (Amendment) Bill 2020](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-essential-commodities-amendment-bill-2020)
- 5 Jun 2020: The Farmers (Empowerment and Protection) Agreement on Price Assurance and Farm Services Ordinance was promulgated — [RajRAS summary](https://www.rajras.in/the-farmers-empowerment-and-protection-agreement-on-price-assurance-and-farm-services-bill-2020/)
- PRS describes each bill as replacing "the June 2020 Ordinance of the same name" — [PRS: Trade & Commerce Bill](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-farmers-produce-trade-and-commerce-promotion-and-facilitation-bill-2020)

### Inferences
- Because the government used ordinances, the laws were in force for about three months before any debate in Parliament. This is a useful "You Be the MP" decision point: ordinance route or bill route?

### Gaps
- The Gazette of India extraordinary notifications for the three ordinances (egazette.gov.in) were not fetched directly.

## Q2. Introduction and passage in Parliament (Sept 2020), the Rajya Sabha voice vote, and committee referral

### Takeaway
All three bills were introduced in Lok Sabha on 14 Sep 2020. Lok Sabha passed them on 15 and 17 Sep. Rajya Sabha passed the two main bills by voice vote on 20 Sep, in chaos, with opposition MPs saying their demand for a division was denied. It passed the EC bill on 22 Sep while most of the opposition boycotted. None of the bills went to a standing or select committee; motions to send them to a Select Committee were not accepted.

### Cited Findings
**Bill-by-bill dates (PRS):**
- Farmers' Produce Trade and Commerce (Promotion and Facilitation) Bill, 2020: introduced in LS 14 Sep 2020, passed LS 17 Sep 2020, passed RS 20 Sep 2020 — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-farmers-produce-trade-and-commerce-promotion-and-facilitation-bill-2020)
- Farmers (Empowerment and Protection) Agreement on Price Assurance and Farm Services Bill, 2020: introduced in LS 14 Sep 2020, passed LS 17 Sep 2020, passed RS 20 Sep 2020 — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-farmers-empowerment-and-protection-agreement-on-price-assurance-and-farm-services-bill-2020)
- Essential Commodities (Amendment) Bill, 2020: introduced in LS 14 Sep 2020, passed LS 15 Sep 2020, passed RS 22 Sep 2020 — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-essential-commodities-amendment-bill-2020)
- Lok Sabha debated the Trade bill for about 5 h 36 min, together with the contract-farming bill, on 17 Sep. In RS on 20 Sep, "several Members" asked for a Select Committee referral, but both bills were passed "amid disruptions" — [PRS Monsoon Session Day 7 update](https://prsindia.org/media/media-updates/monsoon-session-day-7-september-20-2020)
- Union Agriculture Minister Narendra Singh Tomar introduced the bills — [RajRAS](https://www.rajras.in/the-farmers-empowerment-and-protection-agreement-on-price-assurance-and-farm-services-bill-2020/)

**Rajya Sabha, 20 Sep 2020 (voice vote controversy):**
- The bills were cleared by voice vote while the Opposition protested and stormed the Well of the House, demanding a Select Committee referral. The House rejected that demand — [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2020/09/20/par13-rs-farm-bills-pass.html)
- Several MPs sought a Division (a recorded vote), but Deputy Chairman Harivansh Narayan Singh denied it — [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/top-stories/farmers-bills-chaos-rajya-sabha-suspends-8-opposition-mps-163240)
- Derek O'Brien (TMC) went up to the Chair, waved and tore the rule book, and called the bills "black laws". Aides shielded the Deputy Chairman — [The News Minute](https://thenewsminute.com/news/eight-rajya-sabha-mps-including-trinamool-s-derek-o-brien-suspended-133538); [The Wire](https://thewire.in/politics/rajya-sabha-harivansh-derek-obrien-farm-bills)
- 21 Sep 2020: Chairman M. Venkaiah Naidu suspended 8 MPs: Derek O'Brien, Sanjay Singh, Rajeev Satav, K.K. Ragesh, Ripun Bora, Dola Sen, Syed Nazir Hussain and Elamaran Karim. Most sources say the suspension was for the rest of the session — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/politics/rajya-sabha-eight-opposition-members-including-derek-obrien-suspended); [LiveLaw](https://livelaw.in/top-stories/farmers-bills-chaos-rajya-sabha-suspends-8-opposition-mps-163240). Kerala Kaumudi reported "one week", which conflicts with these.
- Naidu rejected a no-confidence notice against Harivansh moved by 12 opposition parties, citing the required 14-day notice period — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Frs-chairman-m-venkaiah-naidu-rejects-notice-for-no-confidence-motion-against-harivansh-890669.html)

**Rajya Sabha, 22 Sep 2020 (EC bill):**
- The EC (Amendment) Bill passed by voice vote while most of the opposition (Congress, Left, TMC, SP, NCP) boycotted over the suspensions. RS passed 7 bills in about 3.5 hours with "barely any discussion" — [The Wire](https://cms.thewire.in/government/after-opposition-boycotts-sitting-rajya-sabha-passes-7-bills-in-3-5-hours); [HuffPost India](https://www.huffingtonpost.in/entry/govt-bill-rajya-sabha-opposition-boycott_in_5f69adc7c5b655acbc6f5303)

**Committee referral:**
- PRS records only "Introduced" and "Passed" stages, with no committee referral. Opposition motions for a Select Committee were not adopted — [PRS Day 7](https://prsindia.org/media/media-updates/monsoon-session-day-7-september-20-2020); [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2020/09/20/par13-rs-farm-bills-pass.html)
- The RSS-affiliated Bharatiya Kisan Sangh also demanded a Standing Committee referral — [Wikipedia (secondary)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Indian_agriculture_acts)

### Inferences
- These events give a strong "You Be the MP" moment: as an RS member on 20 Sep, do you demand a division? Back a Select Committee motion? Join the walkout/boycott on 22 Sep?
- The rules say a member can demand a division after a voice vote. Whether the Chair could deny it because members were not in their seats was the disputed point. The government said the chaos made a count impossible; the opposition said the bills were passed "undemocratically".

### Gaps
- Official RS verbatim debate for 20 Sep 2020 (rajyasabha.nic.in / eparlib.sansad.in) was not fetched. The Chair's exact stated reason for denying the division (reported as members not being in their seats) is not confirmed from a primary source here.
- No source found with PRS's aggregate figure for the share of 17th Lok Sabha bills sent to committees.

## Q3. Presidential assent, the Supreme Court stay (Jan 2021) and its expert committee

### Takeaway
Assent was reported on 27 Sep 2020 (some reports say the President signed on 24 Sep). On 12 Jan 2021 the Supreme Court (CJI S.A. Bobde) suspended all three laws and set up a 4-member committee. The committee filed its report in a sealed cover on 19 Mar 2021, and the report was not made public while the laws were in force.

### Cited Findings
- 27 Sep 2020 (Sunday): President Ram Nath Kovind gave assent and a gazette notification was issued — [Tribune/PTI](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/president-kovind-gives-assent-to-3-contentious-farm-bills-147445); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Indian_agriculture_acts)
- CONFLICT: Deccan Herald reports the President "had signed the Bills on September 24", with gazette publication on 27 Sep — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/national/amid-protests-president-ram-nath-kovind-gives-assent-to-farm-bills-893833.html). Recommended wording: "assented to (gazetted 27 Sep 2020)".
- Opposition leaders had asked the President to return the bills — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/ahead-of-their-meeting-with-president-opposition-mps-take-out-march-against-farm-bills-4461)
- 12 Jan 2021: A bench led by CJI S.A. Bobde stayed implementation of all three laws "until further orders" and named a committee: Ashok Gulati (former CACP chairman), Bhupinder Singh Mann, Anil Ghanwat (Shetkari Sanghatana president) and Pramod Kumar Joshi (agricultural economist, IFPRI) — [The Leaflet](https://theleaflet.in/sc-stays-implementation-of-3-farm-laws-forms-committee); [ETV Bharat](https://etvbharat.com/english/bharat/profile-of-four-member-committee-formed-by-supreme-court/na20210112220142771); [The Wire](https://thewire.in/law/supreme-court-farm-laws-implemntation-stay-committee-talks)
- Mandate: hear farmers and government, make recommendations, and report within 2 months of its first sitting. Bhupinder Singh Mann withdrew soon after he was appointed — [Sabrang India](https://sabrangindia.in/farm-laws-sc-appointed-committee-submits-report/)
- 19 Mar 2021: the committee submitted its report in a sealed cover after 12 rounds of stakeholder meetings. Its contents were not made public at that time — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/sc-appointed-committee-on-farm-laws-submits-report-968628.html)
- Unions criticised the panel because its members had earlier backed the laws — [The Wire (video)](https://thewire.in/law/watch-supreme-court-farm-laws-committee-betrayal-farmers)

### Inferences
- The laws were in force (via ordinances, then Acts) from June 2020 to 12 Jan 2021, then suspended until repeal. They were never implemented again after the stay.

### Gaps
- The Gazette PDFs (Act Nos. 20/21/22 of 2020, per standard citation) were not fetched. Act numbers are unverified here.
- The Supreme Court order PDF (Rakesh Vaishnav v. Union of India, W.P.(C) 1118/2020) was not fetched directly.
- The committee's report was later made public by member Anil Ghanwat (March 2022, widely reported). No source was fetched for this, so it should be treated as unverified.

## Q4. Protest milestones (Delhi Chalo, rounds of talks, deaths, 26 Jan 2021)

### Takeaway
Protests started in Punjab in mid-2020. They became the "Delhi Chalo" march on 26 Nov 2020, and farmers then camped at the Delhi borders (Singhu, Tikri, Ghazipur) for about a year. 11 rounds of talks with the Centre failed. The 26 Jan 2021 tractor rally turned violent, and some protesters reached the Red Fort. SKM said about 700 or more farmers died during the agitation. The government told Parliament it had "no record" of these deaths.

### Cited Findings
- 26 Nov 2020: The Delhi Chalo march began, with farmers from Punjab, Haryana, UP and Uttarakhand. Police used water cannons, barricades and tear gas — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/delhi-chalo-amid-police-face-off-farmers-keep-up-the-march-74413); [Deccan Herald live](https://www.deccanherald.com/national/farmers-protest-live-updates-farm-laws-punjab-haryana-delhi-chalo-amarinder-singh-bjp-congress-modi-tomar-msp-singhu-tikri-931285.html)
- Before 26 Jan 2021, eleven rounds of talks between unions and the Centre had failed — [BBC](https://bbc.in/2Ym2BZz)
- Dec 2020: the Centre offered a written assurance that MSP would continue. Minister Tomar wrote an 8-page open letter saying "MSP and APMC system will continue" — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/dont-fall-prey-to-lies-of-opposition-on-new-farm-laws-agri-min-tomar-in-open-letter-to-farmers-928490.html)
- 26 Jan 2021 (Republic Day): During the tractor rally, groups broke barricades, left the agreed routes and reached the Red Fort. Police used tear gas, and a protester died when his tractor overturned at ITO — [BBC](https://bbc.in/2Ym2BZz)
- Deaths: SKM said "nearly 700 farmers" died in the struggle — [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/india-to-repeal-3-farm-laws-after-months-of-protests/2424988). Down To Earth gives "some 715" — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/samyukt-kisan-morcha-suspends-farm-protests-after-letter-from-centre-80625)
- 1 Dec 2021: The Centre told Parliament it had no records of deaths of protesting farmers — [Bar & Bench (related report)](https://www.barandbench.com/news/farm-laws-repeal-act-2021-gets-presidential-assent); see also [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fpassed-without-discussion-repealed-without-discussion-omars-jibe-over-farm-laws-1055887.html)
- 9 Dec 2021: SKM (40+ unions) suspended the protest after a government letter. The letter said MSP committee would be formed, cases would be withdrawn in several states and compensation paid to families. Farmers left the borders from 11 Dec — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/agriculture/samyukt-kisan-morcha-suspends-farm-protests-after-letter-from-centre-80625); [The Wire](https://cms.thewire.in/agriculture/farmers-at-delhi-borders-begin-packing-as-skm-announces-suspension-of-yearlong-protests)

### Inferences
- The death count is SKM's own tally. It is disputed, and the government has no official figure, so the scenario should label it as "reported by SKM".

### Gaps
- No per-round dates for the 11 talks were fetched. The first round was in Oct/Dec 2020 and the last (11th) on 22 Jan 2021 per common reporting, but this is not confirmed by a fetched source.
- No fetched source on the Lakhimpur Kheri incident (Oct 2021) or the Bharat Bandh dates (8 Dec 2020, 27 Sep 2021).

## Q5. Repeal: PM's announcement and the Farm Laws Repeal Act, 2021

### Takeaway
On 19 Nov 2021 (Guru Purab), PM Modi announced the repeal in a televised address, saying "we couldn't convince a section of farmers". Both Houses passed the Farm Laws Repeal Bill, 2021 on 29 Nov 2021, the first day of the Winter Session, without debate. Lok Sabha took a few minutes and Rajya Sabha passed it by voice vote. The President assented on 30 Nov 2021 per Bar & Bench, with reports on 1 Dec.

### Cited Findings
- 19 Nov 2021: In an address to the nation on Guru Nanak Jayanti, Modi announced the repeal, apologised, and said "we couldn't convince a section of farmers". He asked protesters to go home — [Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/india-to-repeal-3-farm-laws-after-months-of-protests/2424988); [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2021/11/19/1057127412/india-will-repeal-controversial-farm-laws-that-led-to-massive-protests); [EastMojo](https://eastmojo.com/national-news/2021/11/19/protesting-farmers-should-return-home-govt-to-repeal-farm-laws-pm/)
- The announcement came ahead of state elections in UP and Punjab — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2021/11/19/1057127412/india-will-repeal-controversial-farm-laws-that-led-to-massive-protests)
- 29 Nov 2021: Both Houses passed the repeal bill without debate. Opposition parties (Congress, TMC, DMK) demanded a discussion. LS passed it within minutes of introduction, then RS passed it by voice vote, all within about 2 hours — [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/no-debate-parliament-passes-bill-to-repeal-farm-laws-will-answer-all-queries-says-pm-modi-344295); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/lok-sabha-passes-farm-laws-repeal-bill-186522)
- Omar Abdullah's jibe: "passed without discussion, repealed without discussion" — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fpassed-without-discussion-repealed-without-discussion-omars-jibe-over-farm-laws-1055887.html)
- Assent: "received Presidential assent on November 30" — [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/farm-laws-repeal-act-2021-gets-presidential-assent). CONFLICT: Kerala Kaumudi (1 Dec) reports assent "on Wednesday", i.e. 1 Dec 2021 — [Kerala Kaumudi](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/india/general/farm-laws-repeal-act-2021-gets-presidential-assent-697948). Wikipedia gives repeal as 1 Dec 2021 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Indian_agriculture_acts). Likely explanation: assented on 30 Nov and gazetted/reported on 1 Dec. This should be verified on egazette.gov.in.

### Inferences
- There was no debate when the laws were passed (RS) and none when they were repealed. This makes "how much scrutiny is enough?" the main civic question for the scenario.

### Gaps
- Act number of the Repeal Act (commonly cited as Act No. 40 of 2021) was not verified from a fetched source.
- Exact LS passage time was not verified ("4 minutes"/"8 minutes" vary across reports).

## Q6. Arguments for and against (PRS, government, SKM)

### Takeaway
Supporters (the government) argued the laws gave farmers freedom to sell outside APMC mandis, brought private investment and contract-farming certainty, and removed outdated stock limits, while MSP would continue. Opponents (SKM, opposition, several states) feared that tax-free private trade would hollow out the mandis and with them MSP procurement. They also pointed to the lack of any MSP guarantee for private trades, power imbalance in contract farming, dispute settlement by bureaucrats (SDMs) instead of courts, hoarding risk from deregulated stocks, and the Centre legislating on agriculture/markets, which are State-list subjects. PRS was neutral: it said deregulation alone may not attract buyers and that market infrastructure gaps are the binding constraint.

### Cited Findings
**What the laws did (PRS):**
- Trade Act: allows trade outside APMC yards ("outside trade area": farm gates, warehouses, cold storages), allows e-trading platforms, and bars states from levying market fee/cess on such trade — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-farmers-produce-trade-and-commerce-promotion-and-facilitation-bill-2020)
- Contract-farming Act: pre-sowing agreements for 1 season up to 5 years, with a guaranteed price stated. Disputes go to a conciliation board, then the SDM (30 days), then an appeal to the Collector. Farmers' land cannot be attached to recover dues — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-farmers-empowerment-and-protection-agreement-on-price-assurance-and-farm-services-bill-2020)
- EC Amendment: cereals, pulses, oilseeds, edible oils, onions and potatoes are regulated only in "extraordinary circumstances" (war, famine, extraordinary price rise, natural calamity). Stock limits apply only if horticultural prices rise 100% or non-perishable prices rise 50%. Value-chain participants and exporters are exempt — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-essential-commodities-amendment-bill-2020)

**PRS analysis (neutral):**
- Deregulation alone may not attract buyers. In Bihar, after APMC repeal in 2006, market infrastructure eroded, and a 2010 committee of state ministers found deregulation did not bring private investment — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-essential-commodities-amendment-bill-2020)
- 86% of landholdings are small/marginal. The average APMC market serves 496 sq km against the recommended 80 sq km. The Standing Committee recommended a Gramin Haat in every panchayat. APMC prices could serve as benchmarks, and competition could make APMCs more efficient — [PRS](https://prsindia.org/billtrack/the-farmers-produce-trade-and-commerce-promotion-and-facilitation-bill-2020)

**Government's case:**
- "MSP and APMC system will continue". The laws benefit small/marginal farmers, and a written MSP assurance was offered. Tomar said MSP procurement broke records — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/dont-fall-prey-to-lies-of-opposition-on-new-farm-laws-agri-min-tomar-in-open-letter-to-farmers-928490.html); [The Quint](https://fithindi.thequint.com/amp/story/news/india/agri-minister-pens-open-letter-pm-modi-urges-farmers-to-read)
- The Centre can still intervene under the EC Act in extraordinary circumstances — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/national/national-politics/dismaying-coming-from-a-veteran-leader-like-him-tomar-counters-sharad-pawars-tweets-on-farm-laws-945754.html)
- Slogan: "one nation, one market". The Centre said trade and commerce in foodstuffs falls under the Concurrent List (Entry 33) — [IAS Parliament (secondary)](https://www.iasparliament.com/current-affairs/farm-reform-laws-concerns)

**Opponents' case (SKM, opposition, states):**
- Private buyers outside APMCs have no statutory duty to pay MSP. Farmers countered with the slogan "one nation, one MSP". In Punjab and Haryana the government buys over 80% of wheat and rice, so these states had the most at stake — [IAS Parliament (secondary)](https://www.iasparliament.com/current-affairs/farm-reform-laws-concerns)
- Sharad Pawar (NCP): farmers selling outside mandis have no MSP protection with private buyers — [National Herald](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/national/agriculture-minister-not-giving-correct-facts-on-farm-bills-pawar-counters-tomar)
- Federalism: agriculture and markets are State subjects, so the laws were seen as against cooperative federalism. Jairam Ramesh said the EC amendment erodes states' rights — [IAS Parliament](https://www.iasparliament.com/current-affairs/farm-reform-laws-concerns); [HuffPost India](https://www.huffingtonpost.in/entry/govt-bill-rajya-sabha-opposition-boycott_in_5f69adc7c5b655acbc6f5303)
- Contract farming: no price-fixation mechanism, and fear of exploitation by corporates — [IAS Parliament](https://www.iasparliament.com/current-affairs/farm-reform-laws-concerns)
- Process: passed via ordinance, with no committee scrutiny and a voice vote in RS — [The Week/PTI](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2020/09/20/par13-rs-farm-bills-pass.html)

### Inferences
- The opposing sides partly agreed on the facts (MSP stayed as an executive policy). They disagreed over trust and over whether MSP should be legally guaranteed. That disagreement continued into 2024.

### Gaps
- PIB press releases and an SKM primary statement/charter of demands were not fetched. Arguments are sourced from news and secondary explainers, so the original wording should be verified if quoted.
- The civil-court bar in the contract-farming law (Sec. 19) was not confirmed from PRS's page. It is widely reported but unverified here.

## Q7. What happened after: the MSP committee (2022) and the 2024 protests

### Takeaway
On 18 Jul 2022 the Centre set up a 26-member MSP committee under former Agriculture Secretary Sanjay Agrawal, keeping 3 seats for SKM. SKM refused to take part. On 13 Feb 2024, SKM (Non-Political) and the Kisan Mazdoor Morcha started a new "Dilli Chalo" march demanding a legal guarantee of MSP. Haryana police stopped it at the Shambhu and Khanauri borders, where farmers camped for months.

### Cited Findings
- 18 Jul 2022: The committee was constituted with chair Sanjay Agrawal, 26 members and 3 seats for SKM. Its mandate covered making MSP more effective and transparent, natural/zero-budget farming and crop diversification — [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/amp/story/news/india/modi-govt-sets-up-msp-panel-8-months-after-withdrawing-farm-laws); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/skm-rejects-govts-msp-panel-says-so-called-farmer-leaders-who-supported-3-farm-laws-are-in-panel-1128052.html)
- 19 Jul 2022: SKM rejected the panel because it included "so-called farmer leaders" who had backed the laws, along with corporate members. SKM declined to nominate anyone — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/agriculture/skm-rejects-govt-panel-on-msp-says-so-called-farmer-leaders-made-members); [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/agriculture/govt-msp-panel-led-by-farm-law-supporters-is-irrelevant-samyukta-kisan-morcha-83807). (The Wire's "Monday, July 19" is internally inconsistent: 18 Jul 2022 was a Monday.)
- 22 Aug 2022: the committee's first meeting was held, without SKM — [Udayavani](https://english.udayavani.com/article/first-meeting-of-committee-on-msp-to-be-held-on-aug-22/1332591); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/nation/committee-on-msp-holds-broad-discussion-on-key-issues-in-first-meeting-skm-remain-absent-424226/amp)
- 13 Feb 2024: After talks with Union ministers Piyush Goyal and Arjun Munda failed, farmers under SKM (Non-Political) and Kisan Mazdoor Morcha marched toward Delhi, demanding a legal MSP guarantee. Police fired tear gas at the Shambhu border — [Business Today](https://businesstoday.in/india/story/farmers-protest-farmers-break-barricades-at-shambhu-border-cops-fire-tear-gas-417303-2024-02-13); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/farmers-to-continue-delhi-chalo-march-after-inconclusive-talks-with-centre-airport-issues-advisory-417235-2024-02-13)
- The Supreme Court ordered status quo at Shambhu and proposed an expert committee to talk to the farmers — [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/farmers-agitation-for-msp-sc-orders-status-quo-at-shambhu-border-proposes-expert-committee-to-talk-to-farmers-1503324161.html)
- Dec 2024: farmers camped at the Punjab-Haryana borders for over 9 months tried again to march to Delhi — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/india/story/protesting-farmers-break-barricades-at-haryana-border-centre-ready-for-discussions-456428-2024-12-06)

### Inferences
- A legal MSP guarantee was still unresolved at the end of 2024. The scenario can end with an open "what would you propose?" question.

- Whether the Agrawal committee had submitted a final report by the end of 2024 was not found.
- The 2024 death toll and the death of protester Shubhkaran Singh at Khanauri (Feb 2024) were not verified in this research.
